Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Engineering Privacy Standard: Valety is built on a "Zero-Knowledge" architecture. We do not have access to your technical intellectual property.

1. Data Sovereignty & Self-Hosting

Valety operates as a completely self-hosted Windows service. All engineering project files (CAD, Revit, PDF, Excel) remain on your local machine or internal server.

2. Local Database Infrastructure

Unlike standard SaaS tools, the Valety database is self-hosted. All indexed metadata, vector embeddings, and retrieval logs are stored in a local database instance within your infrastructure. No project-specific data is ever synced to a cloud database owned by Valety.

3. Information We Collect

We only collect your email address and basic license metadata to manage your subscription and provide technical support. This data is handled via our secure activation process and is never sold to third parties.

4. GDPR & Compliance

By keeping the database and file processing local, Valety helps your firm maintain GDPR and ISO compliance. Users have the right to request the deletion of their subscription email from our records at any time.
